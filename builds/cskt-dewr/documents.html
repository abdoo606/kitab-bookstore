      How the integration works in production

      • Each opening is a CMS record: title, program, location, type, reference number, posting date, closing date, and the official CSKT Personnel URL.
      • A scheduled check flags postings whose closing date has passed so expired jobs leave the public list automatically instead of misleading applicants.
      • The outbound link carries a clear label and opens in a new tab with a warning, so nobody loses their place on the DEWR site.
      • No applicant data is stored in the DEWR WordPress database — the Tribe's Personnel system remains the system of record.

      Technical Notes Mapped to Addendum #1

      What the RFP now requires, and exactly how this build answers it.

      Q1 · Q19 · Q20 · Q21

      WordPress on CSKT's Managed WP Engine

      The production build targets the CSKT-controlled WP Engine account at $0 hosting cost in the base proposal. Elementor is not required and is not used. I coordinate with CSKT IT five business days before launch for GoDaddy DNS and SSL issuance, whether the site stays on csktwatercompact.com or moves to a new domain. All plugins are GPL-compatible; the custom theme is licensed GPLv2 or later.

      Q13 · Q17 · Q35

      Migration Counts & Zero Stock / Zero AI Imagery

      33 published pages, 3 private pages, 359 media files (199 images, 133 documents, 27 other) with a target of 90%+ migrated plus new program and project archive pages. Legacy PDF tagging/remediation is quoted separately at $12 per PDF. No stock photography and no AI-generated landscapes appear anywhere — only original vector diagrams, and DEWR-supplied photographs with documented rights.

      Q16 · Q17

      WCAG 2.1 AA Minimum, 2.2 AA Targeted

      Visible focus rings, skip links, 44px minimum target sizes, semantic headings, ARIA live regions on every dynamic result, full keyboard operation of the slider, map pins, timeline, and comment form, plus contrast checked at 4.5:1 or better for body text. Honest caveat: full conformance is verified with automated tooling and manual screen-reader testing during Phase 3 — this prototype demonstrates the pattern, not a certification.

      Q18

      Search That Reaches Inside PDFs

      The base proposal indexes page titles, body copy, document metadata (category, program, year, version), and the extracted text layer of each PDF, returning the matched page. Scanned PDFs need OCR and are counted separately; that cost is shown openly rather than hidden in a change order.

      Q28 · Q29 · Q35

      Cultural Review & Language Support

      Every concept, colour, motif, photograph, and Salish/Ksanka string goes to DEWR first, and DEWR routes it to the CSKT Culture Committees. Nothing is published before written approval. Salish and Ksanka render as real Unicode with combining marks, tagged with the correct language attribute for screen readers.

      Q11 · Q9 · Q55

      Contracting, Insurance & Eligibility

      General liability insurance is arranged after selection and before work begins, not with the proposal. The Tribe pays the 3% CSKT contracting fee. I am an independent solo proposer, remote, non-IPO, and filing IRS Form W-8BEN; Addendum #1 Q55 confirms international firms and firms with no minimum size, tenure, or revenue threshold are eligible.

      Honest limitations, stated up front

      • This is a static prototype with sample data. It is not a WordPress installation, and it is not connected to any DEWR system.
      • No accessibility certification is claimed. WCAG conformance is proven by audit during the project, and the audit results are handed to DEWR.
      • No real ArcGIS layers, hydrology feeds, or Tribal records are embedded; every map and metric is an invented placeholder.
      • No prior paid Tribal government contracts, no three-agency reference list, and no Indian Preference certification are claimed.
