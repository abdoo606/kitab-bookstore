Meetings & Open Houses

Addendum #1 Q32 asks for a simple, clear calendar rather than a complex event platform. Each entry shows the date, time, location, type, and — where relevant — the exact public-comment window and whether it is still open.

    What stays simple on purpose

    • No ticketing, no seat booking, no attendee accounts, and no third-party calendar embed that drops a tracking cookie.
    • Each meeting can optionally carry a downloadable agenda PDF and a link straight to its comment form.
    • Past meetings stay published with their materials — the public record is not deleted when a date passes.
    • Staff publish a meeting in one CMS screen; the calendar and the homepage notice banner update from the same record.

    Submit a Public Comment

    Addendum #1 Q31. The form locks itself at the published deadline, accepts a PDF/DOCX attachment, issues a reference number, notifies the assigned DEWR staff member, and gives staff a one-click CSV export of every submission.

    Checking comment window…

    Used only to confirm receipt and to contact you about this comment.
    Minimum 20 characters. Plain text only — no HTML.
    PDF, DOC, or DOCX · up to 25 MB. Nothing else is accepted, and the file is scanned before it is stored.
    Ask Seth Bot a question first

    How the deadline cutoff actually works

    • Each notice stores a closing date and time in Mountain Time. The form compares against that stored value, not a decorative label.
    • When the moment passes, the submit button disables itself, the fields are explained as closed, and the reason is announced to screen readers.
    • Late comments are refused rather than silently accepted, so the public record stays honest about what arrived on time.
    • DEWR staff can reopen a window, extend it, or publish a new notice — every change is logged with who changed it and when.

    Staff export (one click)

    No demo submissions yet — submit the form to test the staff export.

    Privacy & records notice

    • Collected: name, email, topic, comment, optional attachment, and the submission timestamp. Nothing else.
    • Email is used for the acknowledgement and follow-up on that specific comment.
    • Comments are public records. Do not include confidential personal information, medical details, or financial account numbers.
    • Retention follows the Tribes' records schedule; staff can export and archive without losing the audit trail.

    "Seth Bot" — Water Compact Q&A Demonstration

    Addendum #1 Q48. An optional, separately priced assistant that answers only from DEWR-approved sources, prints the citation and page for every answer, and refuses to guess when no approved passage exists.

    Seth Bot (demonstration)

    Approved sources only · citations shown · no legal advice

    Hello. I answer questions about the CSKT–Montana Water Compact and DEWR processes using only DEWR-approved documents, and I show the source and page for each answer.

    I am a demonstration, not an official DEWR spokesperson, and nothing I say is legal advice or a water-rights determination.

    Try one of these: “What is the Compact?” · “Do I have to measure my diversion?” · “How long do I have to submit a comment?”

    Guardrails — why this is not a chatbot that invents things

    • Approved corpus only. Answers come from DEWR-approved pages, FAQs, and public documents. If no approved passage matches, the bot says so and routes the person to staff.
    • Every answer is cited. Document title plus page or section, linked to the public file.
    • No legal advice, no determinations. A permanent notice states this, and the bot never answers "what is my water right?" type questions.
    • No personal data. Visitors are discouraged from entering names, addresses, or account details; the live version logs questions without identifiers.
    • Human escalation. A visible "talk to a person" path appears with every answer, and unanswered questions are queued for DEWR staff.
    • Content owner is DEWR. Adding or removing an approved source is a staff action in the CMS, not a developer release.

    Why it is priced separately

    A safe assistant needs curated sources, a review workflow, and ongoing content maintenance — real work that should be visible in the price rather than buried in a base bid. It is therefore offered as an optional itemised add-on ($950 USD) that DEWR can decline without affecting the core website.

    What Happens After You Submit

    Stated plainly, because a public form should never be a black box.

    Step 1

    Acknowledgement

    An email confirms receipt and shows the reference number, so the submitter has a record even if the browser closes.

    Step 2

    Staff notification

    The assigned DEWR staff member is notified with the topic, the comment, and any attachment — routed by topic, not to a shared inbox nobody watches.

    Step 3

    Public record

    Comments are compiled for the meeting packet and retained per the Tribes' schedule; staff can export the full set as CSV at any time.
