# Proposed sitemap (from RFP Attachment 1) — concept IA

Homepage — "This is the INHT"
  Visual story covering: Locations & Landscapes · Multiple Uses of the INHT ·
  History · Communities (a visitor who only sees the homepage should still
  grasp all four). Primary audiences: outdoor enthusiasts already using the
  Trail; rural communities along the INHT network. Secondary: land
  management agencies.

Plan a Trip
  Maps (interactive) · Visitor Guide · Recreation Info · Passport Program ·
  Auto Tour · Places to Visit · Links to Partner sites

Learn About the INHT
  Records & Stories (existing content) · Explore the History ·
  For Kids (Jr Trailblazer) · For Educators (Professional Development,
  iTREC!) · Project Jukebox · INHT Books (via Store)

Get Involved
  Donations · Membership · Grants · Gateway Communities ·
  Stewardship Work · Workshops · Merchandise (via Store)

About Us
  Staff & Board · Annual Reports & Newsletters · Partners ·
  Media Package · Announcements · Contact Us

Store
  Merchandise · INHT Books · Donations · Membership
  (secure checkout + customer/donor data in the full build)

Cross-cutting requirements noted in Attachment 1: intuitive navigation,
staff-friendly content management, scalability, SEO with clear hierarchy
and internal linking, responsive design, easy email-list collection,
webstore + donations + membership integration, accessibility standards.

Templates built in this concept package: Homepage, Plan a Trip, Get Involved.
Remaining templates (Learn section pages, About, Store) follow the same
header/footer, typography and component styles.
