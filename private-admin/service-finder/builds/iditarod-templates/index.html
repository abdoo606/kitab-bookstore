Iditarod National Historic Trail · Alaska

The Trail for All Seasons

For thousands of years, Alaska Native peoples traveled these routes. Gold-rush stampeders, mail carriers and dog teams followed. Today the Iditarod National Historic Trail still connects communities, landscapes and stories across Alaska — and the Alliance works to promote, preserve and protect it.

About the Alliance

The Iditarod Historic Trail Alliance is the congressionally designated statewide nonprofit for the Trail — working through education, stewardship, interpretation and partnerships with agencies, communities, landowners and volunteers.
