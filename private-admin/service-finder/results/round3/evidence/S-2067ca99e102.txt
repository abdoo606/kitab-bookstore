WEBSITE SERVICES REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS 2026-01
Proposal Name: Website Services
Date Issued: October 5, 2026
Contact Person: Kyle Braun
E-Mail Address: kbraun@siviewpark.org
Proposals accepted until October 23, 2026, 12:00PM by email only to info@siviewpark.org
Si View Metropolitan Park District (SVMPD) is seeking proposals from qualified Consultants to provide website services, including upgrades and improvements, and emergency repairs at www.siviewpark.org.
The scope of work shall include design upgrades to improve and enhance user experience, and ongoing maintenance and backups of the site.
SVMPD desires to move the website to a platform such as Word Press or equivalent, that can be accessed and maintained by Si View Metropolitan Park District staff for ongoing site content updates.
Proposals should be submitted in PDF format by email only to info@siviewpark.org. The subject line for the email shall read "RFP Website Services".
Proposals should not exceed eleven (11) single-sided standard 8½ x 11-inch pages in 12-point type.
Include a list of three professional references, including a summary of the work provided, the name of the organization, main point of contact, phone number and email address (see Attachment A).
ATTACHMENT A: CUSTOMER REFERENCES
Please duplicate form if needed and provide a minimum of three customer references listing customers with similar systems or volume requirements.
INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS: The firm shall maintain insurance that is sufficient to protect the firm's business against all applicable risks (see Attachment C).
BUSINESS LICENSE: The firm awarded the contract will be required to obtain City of North Bend Business License.
Provide copies of all business registrations/business licenses, and Consultant license.
