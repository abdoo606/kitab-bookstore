Five Counties Children's Centre ("The Centre") invites qualified website design/development Vendors to submit a proposal for a full redesign and rebuild of the Centre's public website (www.fivecounties.on.ca).
2.3. Submission Requirements
To be considered, proposals must include the following:
- A complete written or electronic proposal submitted by the deadline to the Centre contact person that addresses the key points and asks in this RFQ.
- All signed and requested information in the Appendices section, including: Appendix A: Vendor Submission Form (including detailed pricing information provided in Canadian dollars) with a binding/authorized signature; Appendix B: References Form; Appendix C: Conflict of Interest Declaration; Appendix D: Vendor Statement of Acknowledgement
- An insurance certificate from the Vendor's Insurer that includes a minimum liability coverage of $1,000,000.
- Proof of AODA compliance (or training) of staff, agents or others involved in the development of the new website (as outlined in this RFQ).
- References from three other website redesign projects that were completed for organizations comparable to Five Counties.
Proposal submission deadline: Oct. 20, 2026, by 4 pm (EDT)
