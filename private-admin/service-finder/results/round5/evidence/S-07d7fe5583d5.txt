Nashwaak Rural Community (NRC) is seeking one primary contractor to handle the redesign and migration of its municipal website along with ongoing website hosting, technical support, managed IT services, Google Workspace administration, cybersecurity, and user help-desk support.
The contractor will support approximately 17 staff and Council users, including: Google Workspace, Gmail and Google Drive administration; Account onboarding/offboarding; Remote help-desk support during business hours; Municipal computers, laptops, printers and standard peripherals.
Provide a 24/7 reporting method for critical cybersecurity incidents or organization-wide outages.
Key Dates
- Proposal Deadline: October 28, 2026
The initial contract runs for three years, with two additional one-year renewal options.
No fixed budget is stated in the RFP
