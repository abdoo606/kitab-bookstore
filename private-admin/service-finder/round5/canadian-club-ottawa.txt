Request for Proposals Website Redesign for the Canadian Club of Ottawa
October 02, 2026
Founded in 1903, the Canadian Club of Ottawa (CCO) has played a central role in convening national conversations at the heart of Canada's political capital.
The Club is seeking a practical, well-executed website that incorporates its refreshed visual identity and can be maintained easily by Club staff.
Core pages, including Home, About/History, Upcoming Events, Past Events/Archive, Membership, Sponsorship and Contact;
An events listing with registration/ticketing integration or clear links to the Club's existing ticketing platform;
Proposals should include: A brief professional profile; Relevant experience and a portfolio of comparable websites; The proposed approach and process, including the recommended content management system and how the Club will participate; Expected timeline covering planning, brand coordination, content migration, development, testing and launch; An itemized fixed price; Any assumptions, limitations, recurring fees or optional costs; and Two relevant references.
As a not-for-profit organization, the Club welcomes nonprofit discounts, in-kind contributions and phased-payment options.
Submission deadline: Wednesday, October 21, 2026
Target selection: By mid-November 2026
Questions may be submitted until 11:59 PM on October 14, 2026.
