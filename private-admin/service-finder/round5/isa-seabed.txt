The International Seabed Authority (ISA), an international organization established under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea, is seeking an experienced digital agency or consortium to redesign and implement its corporate website and content management system (CMS).
Mandatory bidder qualifications
This opportunity is intended for established agencies and consortia rather than individual consultants. Bidders must:
- Be a legally registered organization with at least seven years of continuous operation.
- Demonstrate at least three comparable website, digital platform, or digital transformation projects completed within the past 5-10 years.
- Present a multidisciplinary team covering project management, user research, information architecture, UX/UI, front-end development, CMS implementation, and relevant specialist skills.
Key Dates
- Proposal Deadline: October 20, 2026
No project budget is specified in the RFP
