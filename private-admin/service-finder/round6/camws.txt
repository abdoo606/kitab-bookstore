CAMWS is a nonprofit scholarly association founded in 1905 with approximately 1,100 members.
The selected agency will be responsible for planning, information architecture, visual design, development, content migration, member functionality, conference registration, accessibility, security, performance, training, launch, and post launch support.
Key features include: member portal with login, profile management, membership renewals and member directory; online membership applications, dues payments; conference registration including abstract submissions and payment processing; WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility compliance; backups, malware and firewall protection, multi factor authentication.
