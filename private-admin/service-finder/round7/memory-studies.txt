Memory Studies Association — official call page (fetched 2026-10-20, posted 2026-09-28)
"Open Position: Web Designer/Developer Needed for International Non-Profit Association Website — Deadline: 1 November 2026."
"We are an international non-profit association, legally based in the Netherlands, dedicated to advancing research and supporting scholars both through online activities and through our annual conference. We currently have around 1,500 members from more than 50 countries, with membership expected to grow. Our current WordPress website is no longer fit for purpose, and we're looking for an experienced web designer/developer to design and build a new one."
"We need an up-to-date, secure, GDPR-compliant website that can manage our membership, support communication between members and with the public, and be easy for a non-technical team to maintain going forward."
Core Functionalities: Membership management (annual dues payments and donations); Mail campaigns/newsletters; Members-only area (restricted info + event registration); Public-facing announcements + events agenda; For discussion: integration of existing conference management software such as Dryfta, or new infrastructure (applications, review, registration, payments, programming).
Site Requirements: Fully GDPR compliant; Secure; Clearly organized/consistently navigable; Visually attractive; Accessible across devices (mobile- and screen-reader friendly); EU WCAG compliant; Easy for team to manage; Built on software/hosting based in the EU; Financially feasible to build + maintain; Up and running before 1 July 2027.
What We'd Like From You: brief overview of relevant experience (payments, mail campaigns, member portals); portfolio of past work; recommended platform/approach keeping EU-based hosting/software in mind; estimate of development costs (fixed or hourly + estimated hours); estimate of subsequent annual platform costs; timeline with milestone target dates (design approval, tryout) to launch 1 July 2027; indication of availability for continued maintenance; outline of maintenance costs; indication of willingness to train staff.
"Please send a brief introduction along with relevant portfolio links or samples to info@memorystudiesassociation.org with 'website development' in subject line. Deadline: 1 November 2026."
No references count, insurance, licence or company-registration requirement appears in the call text.
