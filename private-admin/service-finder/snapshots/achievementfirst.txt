Request for Proposals: Website Redesign & Ecosystem Development
Achievement First is seeking an experienced digital agency to architect and build a unified, scalable digital ecosystem. The solution will comprise a main network website, regional hubs, and 41 distinct school-specific websites under a central governance model.
Proposal Submission Deadline | November 9, 2026
Eligible agencies must demonstrate extensive experience in executing complex digital projects.
Three relevant case studies demonstrating multi-site, federated, or enterprise CMS implementations.
In-house expertise across digital strategy, UX/UI, accessibility compliance, and engineering.
Adequate insurance coverage (General Liability, Cyber Liability, Errors & Omissions).
AF anticipates an implementation budget covering discovery, strategy, UX/UI design, front- and back-end development, migration, initial content entry, QA, training, and launch. Ongoing hosting, SLA support, maintenance, software licenses, and third-party tools should be itemized separately.
